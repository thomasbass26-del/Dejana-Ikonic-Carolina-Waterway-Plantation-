The advisor

Fluent in the market,
and in her clients’ language.

Dejana Ikonic represents buyers and sellers from the Coldwell Banker Sea Coast Advantage office at Carolina Forest — the brokerage that has led the Grand Strand for more than forty years.

In February 2026 she was named the company’s individual listing leader for the Grand Strand North region — a distinction earned on listings taken, not on volume bought.

She works in English, Serbian, Croatian and Bosnian. That opens a buyer pool most agents on this coast never reach: Balkan and European families purchasing second homes and waterfront property here, frequently before they ever set foot in the state, and almost always wanting the contract, the survey and the closing explained in their own language.

Her sellers are marketed under a program she calls Every Day Until It’s Sold — the listing works every day it is on the market, not only the week it launches. Her buyers are represented from the first showing through negotiation, escrow, closing, and after the keys change hands.

The approach

Three things a seller
should be able to expect.

Marketed, not listed

A property placed in the MLS is listed. A property with a campaign behind it — aerial film, staging direction, syndication, and a plan for week six as well as week one — is marketed. Only one of those sells at the top of the band.

Four languages, one transaction

English, Serbian, Croatian, Bosnian. Overseas buyers move quickly when the terms are clear to them. For sellers, that is simply a wider bid sheet.

One advisor, start to finish

The person who takes the listing is the person who negotiates it, walks the inspection, and sits at the closing table. Nothing is handed to a coordinator you have never met.
