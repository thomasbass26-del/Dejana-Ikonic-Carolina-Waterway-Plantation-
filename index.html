The community

A hundred custom homes
behind one gate.

Carolina Waterway Plantation sits off River Oaks Drive, near World Tour Golf Resort, on the western bank of the Intracoastal Waterway. Development began in the early 2000s and the first homes were built and sold in 2004.

It is a custom-home community, not a production one — roughly a hundred home sites, each built to its owner’s plans. That is why the streets read the way they do: Mediterranean next to Lowcountry next to something built last year in white oak and microcement. The consistency here is in the lot sizes and the gate, not in the elevations.

A handful of lots are still undeveloped, which is unusual this far into a build-out and is the main reason buyers who want to build rather than buy keep circling back to this street.

On the water

The reason people
buy in here.

Plenty of Grand Strand communities advertise a water view. Far fewer let you put a boat in the water without leaving the gate.

Two private launches

Direct ramp access to the Intracoastal Waterway, inside the gate. No trailering across town, no public ramp queue on a Saturday in July.

A community day dock

Tie up for the weekend without owning waterfront. It is the feature that lets an interior lot live like a waterfront one — and it is priced accordingly.

Fenced boat and RV storage

On-site, secured, and included rather than farmed out to a storage yard on 501. Worth real money against an HOA in this range.

Location

Carolina Forest,
with the water attached.

Six miles to the Atlantic, straight down Highway 501. Minutes to Tanger Outlets, Coastal Grand, and the Carolina Forest medical corridor.

Zoned to River Oaks Elementary, Ocean Bay Middle and Carolina Forest High — River Oaks is close enough that families here treat the school run as a non-issue, which is a meaningful part of why this community holds value against the second-home stock nearer the beach.

Golf is immediate: World Tour Golf Resort is next door, with River Oaks and Myrtlewood a short drive. Coastal Carolina University, Horry-Georgetown Tech, Grand Strand Medical Center and Myrtle Beach International are all inside a twenty-minute radius.
